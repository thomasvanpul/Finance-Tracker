## 2. What Numeris is, and what it is not

Numeris is a place to record and look at your own money: accounts, balances,
transactions, bills, investments, debts, goals and costs you share with other
people, in more than one currency.

**It cannot hold, move, convert or pay money.** Nothing you do in Numeris moves
money in any account you have.

When you connect Wise, Kraken, Alpaca or a bank, Numeris reads your balances and
history from that service. It does not send instructions to it.

Numeris is not a bank, a broker, a payment service or a financial adviser.

## 3. The numbers on the screen

A figure in Numeris is only as right as what you typed in, or what a connected
service sent back.

- **Converted amounts** use European Central Bank reference rates, published
  once a day. They are not the rate your bank or card will give you.
- **Market prices** come from third-party data providers. They can be delayed,
  wrong or missing. Do not trade on them.
- **Net worth, cash and allowances** are arithmetic on the figures above, and
  inherit their errors.

## 4. The AI features

Some features — the chat, page insights, receipt reading and automatic
categories — send information to AI providers. The Privacy Policy, section 5,
says exactly what is sent and to whom.

What comes back is generated by a model. It can be wrong, out of date or
confidently mistaken about your own figures. It is not financial advice. Check
it before you act on it.

## 5. Your account

- Keep your sign-in details to yourself. You can add a passkey or two-factor
  authentication.
- You can delete your account at any time from your profile. You confirm by
  typing your email address. Deletion happens at once and cannot be undone.
- Download a backup before deleting. The backup does not yet include everything
  Numeris holds about you; the Privacy Policy, section 8, lists what is missing.
- Deleting your account does not revoke a token you gave Numeris for Wise,
  Kraken or Alpaca, a Google, Apple or GitHub sign-in, or a bank connection.
  Revoke those in each service's own settings.

`[TO CONFIRM: minimum age. The app does not check anyone's age today.]`

## 6. Other people

Debts and shared expenses let you record other people's names and email
addresses. Those people have not agreed to anything and cannot see what you
wrote.

- Record only what you need to keep track of the money.
- If you enter the email address of someone who has a Numeris account, a
  matching debt, with your description and notes, appears in their account.
  Write it as something you are happy for them to read.
- If a receipt is read by the AI features to split a bill, the names of the
  people you are splitting it with are sent with the photo.
