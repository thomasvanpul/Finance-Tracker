## What differentiates Numeris today (EXISTS only)

Measured against what Monzo, Emma, YNAB and Snoop ship, the distinctive part of Numeris today is small and is mostly in its maths, not in its features. Safe-to-spend by itself is a commodity (Monzo's "Left to spend", Emma, PocketGuard; DIR TL;DR 2). What Numeris adds is how honestly it computes that figure: it shows nothing until it has 7 days of balance history, and it shrinks the figure for balance movement the ledger cannot explain. It also splits a change in net worth into "the exchange rate moved" versus "you moved money", and shows every foreign figure in its own currency first, with a mark saying where the conversion came from. On top of that it has shared expenses that land on another Numeris user's screen and settle between them, and receipt scanning that splits a group bill to the penny. Those two are real, but Splitwise-style apps already cover that ground. The competitor comparison is from the vault notes, not re-verified here. In short: nothing Numeris does today is a feature a competitor lacks outright. The honest edge is multi-currency accuracy (GBP and MYR, native currency first, FX split out from spending) and a safe-to-spend figure built not to over-promise. The things that would make it unmistakably different are all HALF or MISSING: data that arrives by itself, the savings bonus, completeness and confidence per figure, and market movement.
