"Testers open when bank data arrives by itself"

Numeris round 2, 30 Sep 2026. What that phrase means for a tester, day by day.

The gate: nobody is invited until connecting a bank is one tap and spending then appears with no typing, no CSV, no Sync button. It opens when two things are true: the Enable Banking contract is signed (needs you), and the build steps are done (about 3-4 weeks, can start now on your own accounts).
Day 1, 2 min
Sign up, tap Connect a bank, choose Monzo. Monzo's own app asks "share with Enable Banking?". Approve. Back in Numeris: the balance and about 90 days of spending are there, already sorted. Typed: nothing.
Day 2
Coffee at 9:00. Open Numeris at lunch: the coffee is there, dotted until the bank settles it, then solid. A move from Monzo to Wise shows once, as a transfer. Pressed Sync: never.
Days 3-89
The same, every day. The only reason to open Connections is to add another bank.
Day 90, UK
"Keep sharing your Monzo data?" One tap, no bank login. Dutch banks skip this.
Day 173
"Your connection ends in 7 days, Reconnect." 30 seconds, same as day 1. Ignored, the data stops on day 180 and the app says so, never shows stale figures silently.
