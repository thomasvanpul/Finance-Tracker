# Numeris — tester guide (10 minutes)

For the first tester round, week of 28 Sep 2026.

**Link:** https://financetracker.work

Your login is sent to you privately, not in this group — everyone gets
their own account, so there's nothing to mix up between testers. Nothing
here touches your real bank.

## Open it

**iPhone:** open the link in **Safari** → Share → **Add to Home Screen** →
open Numeris from the home screen. (It must be Safari; Chrome on iPhone
cannot install it.)

**Mac or PC:** open the link in any browser.

## Ten minutes

1. **HOME** (1 min). Look at it for five seconds, then close your eyes:
   what do you remember? Say that in the group.
2. **SPENDING** (2 min). Find what was spent on groceries this month.
   Scroll the list back into last month.
3. **Add a spend** (2 min). Tap **+** and add a coffee. Does it show up
   where you expected?
4. **WORTH** (2 min). Find the total across all accounts. Is any number
   confusing?
5. **Mac only: the dashboard** (2 min). What would you look at first?
6. **One minute:** would you open this every morning? Why, or why not?

## Reporting

- **Phone:** on the screen with the problem, **hold the tab bar at the
  bottom for a second**. Write one line, press Send to group, pick the
  testers group.
- **Mac:** press **REPORT** at the top right.
- Figures in the screenshot are hidden unless you tap "Figures hidden".
- One bug or idea per message. Harsh is fine.
